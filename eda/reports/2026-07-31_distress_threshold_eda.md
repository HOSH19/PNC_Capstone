## 4. Recommended thresholds (proposal — not locked)

Chosen to land in the **tens of events / many banks** range, with concentration flagged if one bank owns ≥25% of events. Unit 2 will lock these into a definition doc after review.

| signal | proposed rule | rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit outflow | QoQ ≤ −10% | p5 of deposit QoQ is -0.0359; p1 is -0.0753. −10% sits in the left tail without requiring a near-zero event count. Keeps the role-doc placeholder after checking the distribution. |
| NPL spike | multiple ≥ 1.5× **and** NPL > 2% | NPL p95=2.11, p99=5.48. Level floor avoids noise from tiny NPL bases; 1.5× matches a sharp deterioration. |
| Tier-1 capital | not used (no seed-bank breaches 2017+) | p5=10.45, p1=9.86. Large seed banks stay well above regulatory stress on this series; including a threshold that never fires adds nothing. |
| Liquidity | not used as a hard event rule | p5=7.65, p1=3.14. Low liquidity is informative as a **feature** for Ming's GP, but as a binary event it mostly tags structurally low-liquidity business models rather than acute distress. Defer to features, not the answer key. |
| Unrealized securities loss | not used as hard event (157 quarters / 18 banks at ≤−5%; top-bank share 24%) | p5=-4.55, p1=-7.90. ≤−5% hits 157 quarters / 18 banks (top-bank share 24%). Useful as a **GP feature**, but as a binary event it either floods the label or clusters on a few securities-heavy banks. Keep out of the answer-key OR for v1; revisit in Unit 2 if desired. |

**Proposed event rule (OR):** deposits ≤ −10% OR NPL ≥1.5× and >2%
